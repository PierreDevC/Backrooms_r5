# AGENTS.md — coding-agent rules

Project: The Backrooms: Found Footage, Babylon.js single HTML. Read `HANDOFF.md`, `docs/QA.md`, `docs/LEVEL18.md` and `CHANGELOG.md` first.

- Edit `game/src/*`; build with `cd game && python3 build.py`. Never hand-edit generated HTML or `game/src/assetpack.js` (r5: generated by `game/tools/build_assets.py` from `game/assets`; `game/tools/unpack_assets.py` writes the assets back out).
- r5 assets (docs/ASSETS.md): keep every asset optional (procedural fallback when `B.mdl(...)`/`pbrOpts`/`sknOn()` say no; `?noassets`, `?noskin`, `?nomodels`). A prop swap must make the same RNG draws with and without the model. Skinned rigs keep the procedural rig interface; drive actions through rigPlay/rigWant/rigStop. New third-party assets must be CC0 (or compatible) and listed in docs/CREDITS.md.
- r6 Level 9: `windows9.js` (see-through windows, upstairs portals, LOOK OUTSIDE, deadbolts) and `hack9.js` (PACKET STACK terminal mini-game). Keep `LV.win9` and the wall pieces in step; anything placed on the physical upstairs facade must be flagged `__noPortal` or it will show up in the portal view.
- r7: `state5.js` (Level 5 State Floor and fire lock), `portal5.js` (vestibule inner doors as live portals: anything that must not show through a portal sets `__noPortal`), `moths5.js` (female deathmoths, acid, Mothex on G), `memories18.js` (Level 18 name chain and four rooms), Level 9 `buildCar` (kinds, kerb placement). Tests in `qa_r7/`.
- r6 story layer: `src/story.js` (tasks, documents, FIELD NOTES, hold actions, run flags, `twin()`), `src/places0/9/5/18.js` (each level's places and branches). Write or revise any player-facing words with the `backrooms-dialogue` skill (`.claude/skills/`); keep `docs/STORY.md` (lines, new canon, flags) current. Persistent meshes must not hang off a root that also carries PropBatch parts (finish() disposes those roots): use `twin(root)`.
- Classic-script modules share one strict IIFE. Keep names unique. Build order is authoritative. **main18.js is last and has the only boot() call.**
- Level suffixes: 9, 5 and 18. Shared hooks are `main.js` (both frame and simStep, showEnd, retry, teardown, title, URL entry), `ui.js` (target, foes, HUD, button), and `template.html` (menu). Update every affected hook together. Checkpoints (checkpoint.js, r4.4): call cpSave(label) where level progress is committed, and keep each level's cpRespawn function in sync with new enemies or timed states. main.js frame() isolates sim/render errors via frameErr; do not remove those guards.
- Level 18 reuses hotel geometry/prop helpers and Level 9 door lifecycle. Its fog enemies cannot enter bright cells, CLASS or NAP. Do not break collision/navigation when moving props or story anchors.
- Source state: G18/W18/AI18. Reset and disposal must cover audio, scene, timers, materials, slots and PL.spdK. Keep resource carry-over and minimum values intentional.
- Preserve keyboard/touch controls, subtitles and audio clip parity. New L18 lines are text-only; do not claim voice acting is included.
- Run syntax check and relevant QA. Headless tests use debug-assisted transitions/completion, not human difficulty evidence. Audit actual logs for PAGEERROR, failed assertions or unhandled rejections.

## Every change
Refresh HANDOFF/README/CHANGELOG/status/known limits, AGENTS/CLAUDE if architecture changes, HTML, tests, changed assets and manifest; version the full archive; sync canonical Notion hub/vault/guide pages/work queue; reload pages to verify attachments. Preserve older releases.

If your harness lacks authenticated Notion access, export all updated artifacts and explicitly report **Notion sync pending**. The Notion Skill is not a filesystem watcher.
