FIELD NOTES

  • The Howler hunts by sound and sight. Walk or crouch; sprint only to escape.
  • The Smiler lives where the lights are dead. Keep your beam on it and it retreats.
  • The Crawler only moves when nobody is looking at it. Keep watching.
  • Explorers in hazmat suits may help. Not everything in a suit is human — listen to the voice.
  • Level 9 · The Neighborhood Watch walks the streets with a flashlight. If its beam finds you, get inside a house and shut the door.
  • Level 9 · Wretches sleep inside some houses. They're blind and hunt by sound: crouch near them and keep the NOISE bar left of the amber mark. They open doors, but they can't get past a latched one.
  • Checkpoints — on Easy and Normal, a ◆ CHECKPOINT is saved as you progress (tapes, computers, keys, valves, drawings). If you die you can retry from it and keep what you've done. Nightmare restarts the level.
