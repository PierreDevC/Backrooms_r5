## Premise (wiki: Level 2, "Pipe Dreams")
Dark, hot maintenance tunnels, pipes on every wall and ceiling, steam, the hum of machinery; Facelings (faceless, mostly harmless) and Hounds (crawling, hunt by fear and noise). Here: the tunnels are one pressure system that "surges" every few minutes; between surges you work.

## Spine of the story
1. A phone rings in the sump. IDA (Boiler Office) talks you up the Spine.
2. Ida, back to the door, facing her gauges: the lift is the way up; it wants power and the pressure down; three regulators (west, north, east); the pipes surge, get into a shelter when they knock three times.
3. WEST: the camp of M.E.G. Gamma Team. Crowbar. Logs (who Ida was). Ida's ID card (photo has no face). Turn the west regulator.
4. NORTH: Facelings on shift. The foreman taps the breaker order on a pipe (knock counts, subtitled). Throw the four breakers in that order: power. The foreman leaves a time card on the clock. Turn the north regulator.
5. EAST: the Long Pipe. The Hound: hold your light on it and do not run; turn your back and it comes. BENNY is shut in the valve room (talks through the door); the crowbar opens it; he has the east wheel. Turn the east regulator.
6. Surges stop. The lift: RIDE UP (ending "THE LIFT") or PUNCH IN at the time clock with the foreman's card (ending "ON SHIFT"). Ida's ID card brought back to her changes the ride-up card.

## Systems
- HEAT meter: rises in hot zones (galleries water, long pipe, switchyard), falls in cold rooms, the office, shelters; full heat hurts. Almond water drops it.
- SURGES: warning (three knocks, Ida on every phone, a klaxon), then steam fills every corridor cell that is not a shelter/cold room/office for ~12 s; each regulator weakens them; all three stop them.
- PIPE PHONES: ring when Ida has something to say; pick up for a context hint.
- THE HOUND: noise and fear; light-and-stand-still stare-down; contact damage; retreats.
- FACELINGS: wander, work, turn their heads away from your light.

## Endings
- THE LIFT (ride up; variants: Ida's card, Benny freed, logs read, Level 37 Abara).
- ON SHIFT (punch in with the foreman's card).
Deaths: SCALDED (surge steam), HEATSTROKE (heat), THE HOUND.
