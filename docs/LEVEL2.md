## Premise (wiki: Level 2, "Pipe Dreams")
Dark, hot maintenance tunnels, pipes on every wall and ceiling, steam, the hum of machinery; Facelings (faceless, mostly harmless) and Hounds (crawling, hunt by fear and noise). Here: the tunnels are one pressure system that "surges" every few minutes; between surges you work.

## Rendering (r11)
Level 2 has its own renderer (game/src/rt.js, kit2.js, bake2.js, render2.js):
- Everything static is modelled through kit2.js into tessellated buckets (vertex spacing about 0.4 m on architecture, subdivided pipes and lathed props), so light can vary across every surface.
- At load, a JavaScript ray tracer (a binned-SAH triangle BVH, about 1.5 M rays/s per core) bakes per vertex: direct light from all 168 lamps with soft shadows (bulbs sampled as spheres, fluorescent tubes as lines, high-bays as downward cones), ambient occlusion, and one bounce of coloured light through a radiance cache. The work is spread over a Web Worker pool built from the same functions (about 1.3 s for 800 k vertices on 8 cores; ?nobakeworkers forces the single-thread path).
- Each vertex stores steady light, flickering-lamp light (scaled by the flicker at runtime), the dominant light direction and how directional it is. The shaders re-light the scanned normal maps against that direction, add a GGX highlight from it (wet floors and metal catch the lamps), add the torch and the other dynamic lights, then in-scattering from the eight nearest lamps along the view ray (haze that thickens in a surge).
- Materials are CC0 scans from ambientCG (see CREDITS.md): formwork concrete with a painted dado for the tunnels, plain concrete for rooms, old brick for the culverts, worn floor concrete, diamond plate, perforated walkway, white tile, painted metal, rusty painted metal, worn steel, hazard stripes, corrugated steel. Props are textured triplanar from the metal scans by a material id; glass is drawn blended.
- Characters, doors, items and the sanity meter still read the 2D light texture; bake2 refills it with ray-traced irradiance at 1.1 m (with shadows).

## Spine of the story
1. A phone rings in the sump. IDA (Boiler Office) talks you up the Spine.
2. Ida, back to the door, facing her gauges: the lift is the way up; it wants power and the pressure down; three regulators (west, north, east); the pipes surge, get into a shelter when they knock three times.
3. WEST: the camp of M.E.G. Gamma Team. Crowbar. Logs (who Ida was). Ida's ID card (photo has no face). Turn the west regulator.
4. NORTH: Facelings on shift. The foreman taps the breaker order on a pipe (knock counts, subtitled). Throw the four breakers in that order: power. The foreman leaves a time card on the clock. Turn the north regulator.
5. EAST: the Long Pipe. The Hound: hold your light on it and do not run; turn your back and it comes. BENNY is shut in the valve room (talks through the door); the crowbar opens it; he has the east wheel. Turn the east regulator.
6. Surges stop. The lift: RIDE UP (ending "THE LIFT") or PUNCH IN at the time clock with the foreman's card (ending "ON SHIFT"). Ida's ID card brought back to her changes the ride-up card.

## Systems
- HEAT meter: rises in hot zones (galleries water, long pipe, switchyard), falls in cold rooms, the office, shelters; full heat hurts. Almond water drops it.
- SURGES (r11 rework): warning (three knocks, Ida on every phone, a klaxon, 11 s), then steam fills every cell that is not cover (refuges, cold stores, control room, camp, valve room, lift, pump room) for 11 / 13 / 15 s. Outside cover the steam fills the HEAT bar fast (0.14 / 0.22 / 0.30 per second: full about 4 s after the steam arrives on Normal), and a FULL heat bar burns 8 / 14 / 22 VIT per second (Easy / Normal / Nightmare): a whole surge in the open is survivable on Easy and fatal on Normal and Nightmare. Coughing, a red burn overlay and a flashing bar while it happens. The tunnels' own heat stops at 90%: only steam fills the bar. Each regulator cuts the heating and the duration by a quarter; all three stop the surges.
- PIPE PHONES: ring when Ida has something to say; pick up for a context hint.
- ENERGY BARS and ENERGY DRINKS (r11): pick-ups in the rooms and refuges (Easy 6 + 5, Normal 5 + 4, Nightmare 3 + 2). B eats a bar: half your stamina back, stamina recovers twice as fast for 60 s, +8 VIT. T drinks a can: full stamina, sprinting costs 40% for 40 s. The STA bar glows while either works; the inventory line shows BAR / CAN counts. Counts reset when Level 2 starts.
- THE HOUND: noise and fear; light-and-stand-still stare-down; contact damage; retreats.
- FACELINGS: wander, work, turn their heads away from your light.
