# Level 18 — Nostalgic Memories

## Objective
Arrive → FOLLOW THE PLUSH DINO → FIND YOUR DRAWINGS 0/4 → pin all four at MY MEMORIES → WALK THROUGH THE DOOR YOU DREW → YOU REMEMBERED.

| Drawing | Place | Memory | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOMMY & DADDY | Ball pit, dig while inside | Saturdays at the play place | Sanity hit/white flash |
| BISCUIT | Meadow stump | Gate left open, childhood pet | Sanity hit/white flash |
| MY ROOM | Taped above the bed | Closet monster was a coat | Closet opens; Forgotten emerges |
| OUR HOUSE | Kitchen fridge | Maple Street, a home never revisited | Sanity hit/white flash |

Pinning restores 6 sanity per picture. Drawings can be pinned in batches; carried and pinned HUD counters are separate.

## Dino
Small green plush with stitched belly, feet, tail and button eyes. Leads from hall to Sunshine Room, opens class door and sits on rug. Hugs restore 30 sanity (45-second cooldown); first hug brings one almond water. Nearby Dino restores sanity. On completion it guides you toward HOME.

## Forgotten
Tall thin faceless black silhouette. Spawn when memories start (two on Nightmare), extra on second drawing on Normal/Nightmare, and one from closet. Watches from darkness; fades when looked at long enough; hunts if ignored. A held flashlight beam for ~1.2 seconds dissolves it. Contact costs 25/34/40 HP by difficulty plus sanity. Safe bright cells, classroom and nap room are blocked for its navigation. Respawns after a delay. Threat compass marks nearby real enemies.

## Slides
RED bedroom; YELLOW spiral tube to ball pit; BLUE wave slide to meadow; GREEN tube to preschool. Colour labels and destination pictures accompany each. Interact at the bottom, scripted climb/fade/teleport/low landing. Walkable void paths provide non-slide routes between rooms.

## Sanity and audio
Slow baseline sanity drain, stronger in void; entity fear also affects shared survival. Class/Dino restore sanity. Music box provides +10 when rewound, plays louder for 40 s and cannot be spammed while playing. Twinkle Twinkle rendered offline once in-browser; positional source plus faint bed. Hum in preschool, dark drone in void, toy squeaks/paper/crayon/pins/rustle, distant giggles and whispered regrets.

## Visual references translated into game
Sky-blue corridor/colour doors/drop ceiling/cream tiles; glossy yellow hall; 1–10 playland frieze with balloon/cloud mural, pit and pink slide; painted meadow/turf/rainbow bunny/stump/pond; dim teal slide hall including spiral tube; nostalgic bedroom/kitchen hanging off dark corridors. Geometry/art is procedural, not imported image backdrops.
