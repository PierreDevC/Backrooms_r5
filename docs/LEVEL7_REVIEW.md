# Level 7 review (visual, level design, logic QA)

Tested against a copy of the built game (review7.html, 960x540, Normal difficulty). Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Volumes-crucial-Downloads-Backrooms-r5/e549f242-1f73-4657-828b-a9671490d7ac/scratchpad/qa7v/` (area set), `qa7w/` (weather), `qa7x/` and `qa7z/` (interiors, underwater, hab, flare, shadow). Grid coordinates are (x,z) in metres (cell x 3.6) unless written as cells.
Scripts: `t7y.js` (sequence breaks), `t7z.js`/`t7q.js`/`t7l.js`/`t7f.js` (endings, checkpoint, edges, ladders), plus the existing `t7s.js`, `t7r.js`, `t7c2.js`.

## Verdict
No blockers. Zero page errors / B.ERRS across every run. The full chain completes on all three endings (SAIL, KEEPER, THE DEEP); every ladder, NPC, dial, pickup and ending is reachable (t7r: all true). Out-of-order actions are mostly gated cleanly. Findings below.

## Major
1. **Checkpoint retry does not reset the Gulpers (spawn-kill loop at the Halcyon).** cpRespawn7 (story7.js, last lines) only scares the Shadow and refills breath. Checkpoint "THE MOTOR" is saved inside the wreck at (45,189), where 3 Gulpers roam. Test (t7l.js, wreck-gulper-loop): die to fish, RETRY, fish still "hunt" at distance 0, dead again in about 5 s, three rounds in a row, never survived. Fix: in cpRespawn7, for each AIS7.fish call f.st='lurk', f.aggroed=false and f.place() it 25+ m from PL (same for the Shadow if it is in hunt). Also consider moving the motor checkpoint outside the wreck.
2. **Kell's boat key needs four conversations if you light the lamp early or never "tell"-talked.** talkKell7 plays topic lines in fixed order (lamp, fen, flare) before the key; they are stale after the lamp is lit. Test (t7z.js lit-no-kell-topics): key false after 3 talks, true on the 4th. PS7.told (and so the whole PARTS/FUEL/MOTOR/CABLE/SAFE task block in tasks7) is also only set by Kell's 2nd line, so a player who starts the generator first never sees the task list. Fix: in talkKell7 test `PS7.lit && !PS7.key` before the topic chain, and set PS7.told when the generator, Fen or Wren is met.
3. **Respawn after drowning lands you under water.** cpRespawn7 does not reset G37.ey/under. Death at 3 m depth, retry at the LEVEL START/lit checkpoint (raft, 110,142): 3 s later ey=-1.57 (floor -3.26), still submerged and rising. Harmless at deck checkpoints (ey is recomputed), wrong on open water. Fix: set G37.ey = floorY37+1.62 on land or basin.y+0.22 on water, G37.vy=0, G37.under=false in cpRespawn7.

## Minor
4. **Halcyon log gives away the whole safe code.** story7.js doc 'halcyon': "Four, two, seven." makes Fen's first digit, the mate's log and the tide board redundant. Fix: change to "Chart safe is for the lens motor. Ask the ones I told." and keep the three-source clue chain (dial order also loses meaning).
5. **Ladder prompts overlap on every deck top.** At each rN/rS/rW/rE/sP/sS/sB/cS/cW/pS/pH top (e.g. rS top 110,132) both "CLIMB UP" (2.0 m away) and "CLIMB DOWN INTO THE WATER" (0 m) are ok at once (t7l ladder-top-prompts). story7.js buildStory7, ladder UP interact: ok includes `LV.fh[...] > -1`, which is true for the deck. Fix: require `LV.fh[cell7(PL.x,PL.z)] < 0.5` (or depth37>0.5) for non-pocket ladders so UP shows only from the water.
6. **Generator can be started before meeting Kell** (met=false): objective stays "CLIMB TO THE RIG" while PS7.gen is true, and the generator "weather advisory" lines play before the intro. Fix: ok: PS7.met on the generator interact, or setPhase7 after gen.
7. **Lamp room and bridge room are windowless interiors.** Screenshots qa7x/lamp1-4.png, bridge1-2.png: the lamp room has a good lens cage, panel and yellow pillars, but concrete walls on all sides and no glazing/gallery or sea view; the Aurelia's bridge is a brick room with a table and a dark box (no wheel, windows, chart table). Fix (props7b.js tw room, props7.js br room): add a ring of window openings or emissive "sea" quads, a bridge wheel and console; add a night skybox sprite behind them.
8. **"DON'T TAKE THE LANTERN" toast fires after leaving the wreck.** places7Events: `later(2, ...)` is not re-checked against the zone; it also shows in the lamp room if you swim away quickly. Fix: re-test `z === ZS7.WRECK || ZS7.POCKET` inside the callback.
9. **Habitat/wreck/trench water is very dark and featureless without the flashlight** (qa7x/fen1.png, trench.png, qa7z/seabed.png; fog den 0.05, colours around 0.03-0.1). Props are readable only inside the flashlight cone; on the seabed there is no floor texture or marker. Fix: raise zoneLook7 HAB/WRECK ambient to about 0.09-0.16 and lower den to 0.035; add a few emissive markers (guide lights along the cable to the habitat).
10. **Raft occupies half the screen at the start** (qa7z/flare.png, stormsea.png: a flat orange slab fills the lower half of the view at ey 0.2). Looks like a scaling or camera-low issue on the raft model; shrink or lower the raft or raise ey to about 0.5 on it.
11. **Flares can be fired underwater** (code read, not play-tested): useFlare7 in main7.js has no G37.under check. Flare spam itself is fine (30 in a row, 3 s cooldown, no errors). Fix: return early with a toast when G37.under.
12. **Shadow damage curve is steep**: 42 per bite on Normal with a 7 s flee (hunt test: 100 to 58 to 23 in about 30 s). Gulper: 13 per 2.5 s ticks, 100 to 48 in 8 s. Consider 30/9 on Normal.

## Checked and fine (no bug)
- Premature actions: splice w/o clamp/gen, lamp panel with no parts, motor before safe (not ok), valve w/o power, fill w/o can, Marlin in the dark/without key, cable before lit, Fen before air: all toast/no-op; no state change (t7y s1-premature).
- Dials: spin to 4-2-7 opens the safe in any order, further turns after opening do nothing, motor takes once. Seating parts six times: caps at 3/3 and does not light. Reading docs 6x: no errors.
- Ending guards: win7 only fires from 'play'; 3x followCable7 / keep7 / BOARD spam gives one ending; THE DEEP before lit only toasts; KEEPER needs wrenLit and nearness.
- Dying mid-hold (generator at 25%): hold cleared, label resets, generator not set; retry works. Retry after lamp lit: PS7.lit kept, Shadow gone, no errors.
- Map edge: positions beyond the grid clamp to 0 or 230 m; swimming east stops at an invisible wall near x=216, no fall-out or error.
- Weather/time of day: storm and dawn read well on the rig deck (qa7w/w_storm, w_dawn); buoys and the pier look correct; Fen on his platform with desk and lamp reads well (qa7z/fenplat.png).
